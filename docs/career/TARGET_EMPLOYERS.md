# Target Employers - Purple Team / CTI / Detection Engineering

Career targeting document for post-graduation job search.
Focus: Florida primary, Texas secondary, California tertiary.
Target roles: Threat Hunter, Purple Team Operator, CTI Analyst, Detection Engineer.
Clearance preference: Non-cleared commercial roles only.

---

## Why Commercial Non-Cleared

Commercial roles hire on demonstrated skill - your GitHub portfolio, Sigma rules,
Diamond Model reports, homelab architecture, and Black Hat scholarship are all
directly evaluable by a hiring manager. That is your strongest hand.

Cleared roles hire on background first, skill second. The clearance process
takes 6-18 months, limits employer options, and often means lower base pay.
Commercial purple teaming is also where the most interesting work is happening
right now - AI security, adversary emulation, and detection engineering are
almost entirely commercial.

## What Hiring Managers Look For

1. Telemetry and MITRE ATT&CK alignment
   Hands-on mapping of adversary TTPs to endpoint/network telemetry
   EDR logs, Sysmon, KQL/SPL, PCAP - not high-level policy knowledge

2. Detection Engineering output
   Converting hunt findings into permanent detection rules
   YARA, Sigma, Snort, EDR queries - mention these explicitly on resume

3. Practical purple team experience
   Evidence of running real exercises not just theoretical knowledge
   Matrix homelab exercises carry significant weight here
